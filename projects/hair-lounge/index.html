Aurea Salon & Studio

Winnipeg — High-end salon experience

An award-worthy cinematic redesign focused on storytelling, booking conversions, and brand prestige. I used motion, layered depth, and interactive moments to make the site memorable and performant.

Project Goal

Create an ultra-premium, fully animated website with cinematic storytelling, premium motion graphics, 3D cutouts and immersive interactions — blending Apple, Awwwards and luxury fashion cues.

Design Principles

  • Ultra-premium visuals with generous whitespace
  • Smooth scrolling & page transitions
  • Mobile-first and accessibility-aware
  • High-conversion CTA & booking flows

Visual Strategy

Dark & light mode support, glassmorphism, layered depth, large cinematic typography and subtle gold accents for a luxurious feel.

Key Animations

  1. Logo reveal and loader progress
  2. Scroll-triggered reveals & parallax
  3. Mouse-reactive 3D hero scene
  4. Staggered gallery entrances
